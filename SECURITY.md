# Security

If you find a way to read a sealed request or reply outside the enclave, to make the enclave run code that [morse-enclave](https://github.com/trymorse/morse-enclave) does not build, or to make the app accept an image that `enclave_pins.json` does not list, report it privately. Do not open a public issue.

Report it through this repository's **Security** tab, under **Report a vulnerability**, or by email to hello@trymorse.ai. Say which tag you looked at and how to reproduce the problem.

We aim to acknowledge reports within 3 business days. We tell you what we found, and when a fix ships we credit you in its release unless you ask us not to.
